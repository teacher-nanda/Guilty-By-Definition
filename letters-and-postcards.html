🔓 Decoded

The first words of each paragraph spell out an acrostic: "This Time You Have Made Two Maidens Cross." (Page 28)

"Two maidens and a cross," read as Roman numerals, gives MMX — the year 2010. "Chorus" is Old English for "voice of freemen" — the root of the name Charlotte. The letter is about Charlie.

🔓 Decoded

Hidden inside: a "time signature" written as a fraction — a signal to count on through each paragraph. It points to a real 17th-century prisoner: ANTHONY SEDLEY, 1649, PRISNER — carved into a church font in Burford, where Leveller prisoners were held and executed during the Civil War.

That trail leads straight to Brin Edwards, the Burford book dealer Charlie once worked for.
