💭 Big-Picture Discussion Questions

For the whole group — after finishing Chapter 13

1. Truth vs. comfort. The letters keep repeating "Truth will come to light." Is Martha better off knowing the truth about Charlie, even if it's painful — or would a comforting story be kinder?
2. Two sisters. Martha calls herself Charlie's "shadow." How does the book show two very different ways of dealing with life — Charlie's boldness vs. Martha's quietness?
3. Was Charlie likeable? Several characters admit they didn't really like Charlie, even though "everyone loved" her in public. Why show us these mixed opinions instead of a simple, perfect victim?
4. Language as detective work. The team solves clues using etymology, acrostics, and Shakespeare quotes. What does the book suggest about words hiding — and revealing — the truth?
5. Who is Chorus? Based on the clues so far, who do you suspect Chorus is — and why?
6. Grief and rituals. Martha's mother kept Charlie's room untouched. Her father can't let go of it either. How does the book show grief without a body or an ending?
7. Predictions. Chapter 13 ends with the team driving to Burford to meet Brin Edwards. What do you think they'll find there?
